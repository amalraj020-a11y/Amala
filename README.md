## About

Amala is a personal AI assistant concept focused on helping with everyday productivity, information organization, planning, and digital workflows.

The goal is to make technology more useful through clear communication, practical automation, and a thoughtful user experience.

> **Mission:** Turn complex digital tasks into simpler, more organized experiences.

## What Amala Focuses On

- **Personal productivity** — organizing tasks, priorities, and plans.
- **Research and information** — helping gather, structure, and summarize information.
- **Workflow automation** — reducing repetitive digital work.
- **Creative support** — helping develop ideas and content.
- **Digital organization** — making information easier to manage and act on.
